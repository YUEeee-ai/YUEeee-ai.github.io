Research

WHAT I WORK ON

Opponent Screening in the Iterated Prisoner’s Dilemma

In repeated games, an agent must quickly identify the behaviour of an unknown opponent and adapt accordingly. In this project I designed a lightweight trigger–probe–classify decision template: the agent reveals the opponent’s type through probes, classifies it online with statistical tests, and switches to a suitable response policy as the match unfolds.

Evaluated against all 244 strategies of Axelrod 4.14.0 (200 turns × 3 matches per opponent), the template reached a full-library mean score of 2.845; with sequential-test upgrades it improved to 2.913 while keeping its retaliatory strength. The work is documented in a paper (in preparation).
